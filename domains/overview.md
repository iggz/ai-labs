# AI Labs Overview

## Project Goals
AI Labs is a premium web suite containing computer vision tools to analyze physical movement and body sizing.
- **FormAI Coach:** Computer vision feedback on workout exercises (squat, deadlift, hip thrust) with neon skeleton overlays and tempo/symmetry measurements.
- **SlingShot:** Velocity trails and barbell tracking to output shareable, vertical lift videos.
- **SmartFit Guide:** Sizing recommendation based on body proportions from full-body silhouettes.
